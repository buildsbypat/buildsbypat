## SpaceBuddy | The idea in a nutshell

SpaceBuddy will be focused around giving people a better look at space without the need for fancy telescopes and such. My plan consists of building a central source of information from sources such as NASA, Google Earth Imagery, CelesTrack and more. My plan in the end is to roll out an iOS app by the end of the year to early 2027. With plans to create a discord community and creating a Discord Bot for things like Photo of the Day from NASA and news from NASA and the Russian Space Agency.

More information on the plan for SpaceBuddy will be available soon.
